Features

FireReady

Centralizes recurring tasks, reminders, and documentation homeowners need to harden their homes before wildfire season. Includes checklists, progress tracking, insurance document storage, and evacuation planning.

SparkSafe Learn

A mobile-first learning hub delivering bite-sized, actionable wildfire preparedness modules. Includes myth-busting lessons, micro-videos, and a simulation that visualizes local fire risk.

FireReady Score

Converts scattered preparedness actions into a single 0–100 readiness index. Shows progress, highlights lagging areas, and allows fire marshals and insurers to validate actions.

FireSmart Assessment

Fast, guided home risk assessment. Generates a prioritized, actionable to-do list for home-hardening. Supports documentation for insurance verification and seasonal reminders.

FireHardening

Step-by-step checklist for home-hardening tasks with vetted product recommendations and links to retailers or contractors. Aligns homeowners, fire marshals, and contractors in a single workflow.
