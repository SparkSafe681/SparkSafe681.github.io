About SparkSafe

SparkSafe is a mobile-first preparedness and community coordination platform designed to help fire departments, municipalities, retailers, insurers, and neighborhoods operationalize home-hardening and evacuation readiness before wildfire season.

Our mission is to transform wildfire preparedness from reactive to proactive by giving homeowners clear guidance, tools, and support to protect what matters most. We aim to reduce fear, confusion, and preventable losses, while enabling communities and authorities to track and improve resilience together.

By connecting verified guidance from fire authorities with actionable workflows and trusted home-improvement partners, SparkSafe makes it simple for families to prepare, act, and recover — creating measurable impact before, during, and after wildfires.
